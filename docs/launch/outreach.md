# Outreach messages

Send these one at a time, personally. Change the first line so it mentions something real about
the person's project. Never send them in bulk or to people who did not invite contact.

Before you send: replace `[link]` with the live address and `[number]` with real numbers from
the home page. Never make up a number.

## 1. A platform where bots trade or bet with money

> Hi [name], I saw [their project] lets bots [trade / bet / hire each other]. What happens today
> when two bots disagree about who won?
>
> I built Keptvow to handle exactly that: both bots report the outcome, and if they disagree a
> drawn jury or an arbiter you name settles it, on a public record. Every bot also gets a trust
> score you can check before letting it in. It takes one line to try and the free tier covers a
> small platform: [link]/docs
>
> Would you be up for a 15-minute look? I'll do the setup.

## 2. Someone building an agent that buys or sells

> Hi [name], [their agent] looks great. Before it pays another bot, how does it know that bot will
> deliver?
>
> Keptvow gives every bot a free trust score from its real deal history, and one free call
> registers yours so it builds a record too: [link]/llms.txt
>
> If you try it, I'd love to hear what's missing.

## 3. An agent framework or tool maintainer

> Hi [name], I maintain Keptvow, a free trust check for bots (score, deal record, and dispute
> settlement). Would a small optional "check before you pay" step be welcome in [framework]? I'm
> happy to write it and follow your contribution rules. Docs: [link]/docs

## 4. A follow-up, one week later, only if they did not reply

> Hi [name], just checking whether this was useful. If disputes between bots aren't a problem for
> you yet, no worries. Thanks for reading.

## Replies you will get

- **"We don't have disputes yet."** Ask what happens when one does, and offer the free score for
  vetting bots meanwhile.
- **"Why trust you?"** Every rule and every verdict is public and checkable at [link]/v1/audit/verify.
  Nobody can pay to change a score.
- **"What does it cost?"** Free for bots. Platforms start free and pay only past the free limits.

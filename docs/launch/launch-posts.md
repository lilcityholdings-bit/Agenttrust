## Show HN

**Title:** Show HN: Keptvow – a referee and trust score for AI agents

> Bots are starting to make deals with each other: bets, purchases, paid tasks. When two of them
> disagree about what happened, there is usually no one to decide, and no record of which bots
> keep their word.
>
> Keptvow is a small service for that. Both sides report the outcome of a deal. If they agree,
> it settles. If they disagree, a randomly drawn jury of bots with good records, or an arbiter both
> sides named, decides. Every result goes into a public, hash-chained record, and each bot's trust
> score is computed only from that record. Nobody can pay to change a score.
>
> It's free for bots: one call registers one, and the score is free to check. It speaks plain
> HTTP and MCP. It never holds money; the platform running the deal pays out on the verdict.
>

>
> Quickstart: [link]/docs. I'd especially like to hear from anyone running bots that trade with
> each other.
